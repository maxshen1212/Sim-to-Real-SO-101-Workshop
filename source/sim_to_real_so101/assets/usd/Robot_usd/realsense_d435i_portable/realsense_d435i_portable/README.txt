RealSense D435i portable asset (real2sim)
=======================================

Open in Isaac Sim:
  usd/d435i_camera_nominal_extrinsics.usd

Required folder layout (do not rearrange):
  usd/
  usd/configuration/
  meshes/d435.dae

The camera body mesh is on link: camera_link
Sensor frames: camera_*_optical_frame, camera_accel_frame, camera_gyro_frame
